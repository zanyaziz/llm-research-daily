Tuesday edition · Frontier launches, agent safety & the evaluation turn

Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 takes the top benchmark spot as Meta ships its cheapest agent model

A big model-release day and a thin pure-paper day: two frontier launches from Anthropic and Meta, NVIDIA's robot foundation model, and Google's new TPUs — while the arXiv crop was dominated by agent-safety and evaluation work rather than new training breakthroughs.

One model, two safeguard settings. Anthropic's new flagship pair — Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 — shares a single underlying model, split by guardrails: Fable is broadly available, while Mythos, with looser cyber/bio safeguards, is restricted to organizations passing Anthropic's verification programs. Anthropic reports outputs up to 30% faster than 5.1, a 20% price cut to $8/$40 per million tokens, ~55% fewer false safeguard blocks, and a showcase demo in which ~1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean in 30 hours. Within hours, Fable 5.2 took first place on the independent Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.

The volume play. Meta's Muse Ember is the opposite bet: the smallest Muse model yet, at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens, built to execute high-volume repetitive agent work under Muse Wonder and Spark. It scores 36 on the Artificial Analysis index — level with Claude Haiku 5.5 — at roughly a third of Haiku's cost per task, completing Meta's three-tier Muse stack. Note the dating: Muse Wonder launched on Oct 12, 2026, not Nov 9, despite one Nov 10 roundup's claim.

The paper crop. The research story of the day is agent safety as a systems problem: web-agent security turns out to depend as much on the harness as the model (32.55% attack success across six open harnesses), every tested agent memory system proves poisonable in MemoryDojo, and prompt-injection guards are failing on legal contracts with no published false-positive rates. The strongest evaluation result: the best single AI peer-review system catches 71 of 100 planted errors — pooling all systems catches 93.

Lead story sources: Reuters (Anthropic launch) and Digital Trends (Muse Ember) — links in the Top papers section.
