Friday edition · System One decision models & the agent shipping day

Gemini 4 Hydrogen goes live in Search as Google, OpenAI and Anthropic all ship for agents

November 12 was a model-and-agents news day rather than a blockbuster paper day: Google made Gemini 4 Hydrogen generally available and put it behind all of Search, Thinking Machines open-weighted Inkling 2 Small under Apache 2.0, OpenAI launched its Daybreak security-agent products, and Anthropic opened self-hosted Claude Managed Agents. The strongest papers cluster around one theme: fast “System One” decision models — how to build them, evaluate them, and trust them.

Hydrogen everywhere, at once. Gemini 4 Hydrogen — Google's fast Gemini 4 model, adapted from Gemini 4 Carbon — went GA on the Gemini API, including the free tier, and became the default model for Google Search, the Gemini app, Antigravity, Cursor and Devin on the same day. It carries a 1M-token context, flat pricing of $1.50 / $7.50 per million input/output tokens, and four thinking levels. Google positions it as the everyday coding and agentic workhorse below frontier Carbon and Argon.

Open weights and agent infrastructure. Thinking Machines Lab published the Inkling 2 Small weights (594B total / 17B active MoE) on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0, a week after its hosted launch. OpenAI launched two Daybreak security-agent products — Red Team and Defense Factory — on GPT-6 Cyber, and Anthropic opened a private beta for self-hosted Claude Managed Agents, letting businesses run the harness, sandboxes, credentials and session history on their own infrastructure.

The paper theme: System One. Three of the top four papers are about fast decision models. SO-Arena benchmarks 21 of them and finds the accuracy leader ranks only seventh on reliability; a single-author paper shows how to convert an LLM into an adaptive System One model that spends depth only where needed; and a Zhejiang University study finds decision models that fail as actors can excel as judges and teachers — lifting a small GRPO policy by ten points on ALFWorld. Alongside them, the day's best interpretability result localises goal encoding in LLMs to a sparse set of attention heads, with causal tests.

Lead sources linked in the sections below: Google (Gemini 4 Hydrogen); Thinking Machines Lab (Inkling 2 Small); Anthropic (self-hosted Claude Managed Agents); arXiv 2611.15777, 2611.17297, 2611.16321, 2611.17167.
