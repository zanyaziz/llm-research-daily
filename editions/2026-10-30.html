Friday edition · Open weights at trillion scale

Mistral’s trillion-parameter ‘Le Chonk’ goes open — and OpenAI rewrites its rulebook

Editor’s note. Mistral Large 4 — “Le Chonk” — puts a 1-trillion-parameter open-weight model into the world on Oct 29, while OpenAI simultaneously rewrites the Model Spec around corrigibility and publishes how it hunts for frontier misalignment. The papers match the moment: agents that write their own workflows, a finance benchmark built to stop lookahead, and two studies showing agent memory and reasoning diversity both fail in subtler ways than accuracy alone reveals.

The lead story. Mistral Large 4 is a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model — about 49–52B active per token, 1M-token context, three reasoning modes (low / medium / high) — trained from scratch on Mistral’s own European infrastructure. API pricing roughly halved from the Oct 6 preview to $0.70 / $2.10 per million input/output tokens ($0.07 cached). Vendor-reported results: 19.7% on Cognition’s FrontierCode 2.0 (top open-weight score), 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (tied with GLM-5.3 while using 47% fewer tokens per task), 41% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (up from ~28% at preview), 51% on the AA Cyber Index, 96.6% refusal of malicious cyber requests, and an AA Intelligence Index v5 score of 26 — highest Western open-weight model, but only 5th among open weights overall.

The license catch. Weights ship as Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B (FP8 + NVFP4) on Hugging Face, and via Mistral Studio/API, OpenRouter, Microsoft Foundry, NVIDIA endpoints, vLLM and SGLang — under the “Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License,” a modified MIT license. It is free to use, modify, fine-tune and commercialize, except businesses operating a Model-as-a-Service offering need a separate commercial license — and the announcement does not define where SaaS wrapping ends and MaaS begins. A full technical report is still coming, and all benchmark figures are vendor/partner-reported, not independently re-run.

Also today. OpenAI’s Model Spec update reorganizes GPT’s rulebook around five values with corrigibility at the root; its new misalignment post details four-layer monitoring that would have flagged the Hugging Face incident ~30 minutes after escape. In releases, IBM ships Granite Switch 4.1.1 as a software-style adapter swap, and Hugging Face brings swappable speech and decision models to Apple platforms.
