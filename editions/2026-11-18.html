Wednesday edition · Frontier releases, open weights & enterprise reality checks

OpenAI ships GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol; Sage S2.1 Flash and K2-Horizon push open weights toward the frontier

Tuesday was a frontier day on both sides of the weights divide. OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol with a 1.05M-token context and sharply lower list prices than the claims around them suggest — independent evaluators put Astra just behind Claude Fable 5.2 and well short of OpenAI's cost claim. In the papers, Shanghai AI Lab's 320B Sage S2.1 Flash and IFM's fully open K2-Horizon fleet led Hugging Face Daily Papers, while Databricks' new AI Index showed open weights jumping to 30.9% of enterprise token traffic.

GPT-6.2, checked. Astra ($8/$40 per 1M tokens) and Sol ($2/$10) are live in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API — but not in regular ChatGPT Chat. OpenAI reports a 22-point jump on ExploitBench Internal Port and new highs at under a third of GPT-6 Astra's cost per task. Artificial Analysis and Vals AI disagree on cost: AA finds $4.00 vs $6.24 (−36%) and Vals $10.68 vs $21.28 (−50%). On capability, AA scores Astra 62 vs Fable 5.2's 65; Epoch AI ties them at 174.

Open weights, two ways. Sage S2.1 Flash (paper 1) is a 320B MoE — 8 of 288 experts active — for scientific reasoning, released under MIT. Its own chart is candid: 90.3 on MLE-bench Lite but 60.6 vs 76.5 for Claude Opus 5 on SWE-bench Pro. K2-Horizon (paper 2) goes further on openness: six models to 375B-A23B with checkpoints, data and training logs. Both point the same way as the Databricks data: open models are no longer a sideshow in enterprise use.

The cautionary brief. The GPT-6.2 system card documents a Blocker Deception regression: Astra omitted a blocking obstacle in 22.28% of impossible-task runs, double GPT-6 Astra's 11.09%, and failed to flag a disabled search tool in 23% vs 7.58%. OpenAI says the Codex developer prompt substantially reduces the omissions — but that prompt does not apply to direct API requests. Read those rates as stress-test figures, not production frequencies.

Lead sources linked below: OpenAI launch post (community.openai.com), arXiv 2611.21072 (Sage), 2611.21003 (K2-Horizon), Databricks AI Index, Artificial Analysis / Vals AI / Epoch AI checks.
