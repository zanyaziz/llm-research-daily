Friday edition · Open-weight week

Inkling 2 Small leads a crowded open-weight week — and a paper argues you don't need long context at all

November 5 was unusually heavy on releases — Inkling 2 Small, GPT-6 Cyber, Kimi K3.1 Pro weights, Hy4, Ling-3.2-flash, Cosmos 3 and Ripple-65B — plus METR's independent assessment that Anthropic's AI R&D is accelerating ~2×, and a coherent cluster of agent-RL papers converging on turn-level credit and error recovery.

Editor's note: A strong day — unusually heavy on model releases plus the METR Anthropic assessment, and a coherent cluster of agent-RL papers converging on turn-level credit and error recovery. No new announcements found from Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, or Mistral in this window beyond the METR assessment and the Gemini Omni listing.

The lead paper challenges a core assumption. NVIDIA's “You don't need long context” finds later tokens cost up to ~7× more than earlier ones and that frequent compaction sacrifices little accuracy — proposing semantic state space models that carry a compact natural-language state instead of full history.

The release story needs care. Inkling 2 Small's weights are promised next week pending safety testing, not yet out; Kimi K3.1 Pro's 2.8T weights are out; Hy4's weights are still promised “in the coming days.” Vendor-reported scores dominate this edition and are labelled as such throughout.

The policy story is METR. After an 11-week high-access evaluation, METR estimates Anthropic is improving capabilities on well-specified tasks at ~2× its March 2024–October 2025 rate — with wide uncertainty (1.3×–3× plausible) and heavily redacted evidence.

⚠ Caveats — how to read this edition

  • Paper 11 is unverified: “Benchmarking LLMs on Heterogeneous Cognitive and Biomedical Knowledge Graphs” rests on secondary coverage only — arXiv ID and author list could not be confirmed. Treat as unverified.
  • Vendor-reported dominates releases: Inkling 2 Small, GPT-6 Cyber, Kimi K3.1 Pro, Hy4, Ripple-65B and others report their own scores. Inkling's weights are promised next week pending safety testing (conditional, not guaranteed); Hy4's weights were promised “in the coming days” and were not yet public at launch.
  • Fresh preprints: The lead long-context paper (2611.05306) has no independent replication; its accuracy experiments use closed models (Claude Opus 4.5, GPT-5.6 Sol) and its SSSM is a proposal, not a trained model. LoCo's abstract names no base models and publishes no per-benchmark accuracy numbers.
  • Secondary-only items: Ant Ling-3.2-flash details, Baidu ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, InteractionBench, and the China export-catalogue flag rest on secondary coverage only. Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is a listing-only appearance on OpenRouter, not a confirmed launch.
  • METR uncertainty: METR's ~2× acceleration headline is a subjective estimate with wide uncertainty (1.3×–3× plausible; AECI 1.9×, 95% CI 1.5–2.8×) and heavily redacted underlying evidence — Anthropic redacted a majority of the evidence METR found most informative.
  • No announcements: No new announcements found from Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, or Mistral in this window beyond the METR assessment and the Gemini Omni listing.
