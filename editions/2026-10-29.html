Thursday edition · The benchmark leader you cannot use yet

Google’s Gemini 4 Carbon takes the top of the benchmark table — but you still can’t use it yet.

Editor’s note. Google DeepMind’s Gemini 4 Carbon, announced October 28, tops Artificial Analysis’s Intelligence Index v5.0 — yet access is deliberately slow, with trusted cyber defenders first and no general app or API date. The day’s papers were just as pointed: a top-voted spatial-reasoning paper on “interface lock-in,” a warning that agent memory can be worth less than plain search, and a six-paper cluster showing coding agents failing in ways the usual metrics miss.

The lead story. Carbon scores 59 on Intelligence Index v5.0, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 (58), GPT-6 Astra (57) and Gemini 4 Argon (50) — a one-point lead, and it trails Opus 5.5 on the Coding Agent Index, 59 to 62. Google’s own security figures are strong — 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, 54.8% on ExploitGym, 71.0% on CWE-bench — but they are vendor-reported, and rollout runs through trusted defenders and the Fairwind Program first.

Also today. ChronoReplay (131 upvotes, the day’s top Hugging Face paper) argues one answer format starves the others; Memory Tax finds dedicated memory systems losing to naive RAG over raw history; and NVIDIA’s open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe packs transcription, diarization, translation and audio QA into one checkpoint. EmbeddingGemma 2 also resurfaced — it is not a new launch, and is labelled as such below.

Read with care. Vendor-reported numbers are labelled throughout. Two briefs rest on secondary coverage only, the distillation and GLM-5.3 items are reported — not confirmed by the labs — and Mistral Large 4 weights are still pending, with a Hugging Face countdown pointing to October 31, 15:00 UTC.

⚠ Caveats — how to read this edition

  • Preprints: All papers above are preprints unless stated otherwise and have not been peer-reviewed.
  • Vendor-reported: Vendor-reported benchmark numbers are labelled as such throughout and are not independent verification — including Gemini 4 Carbon’s security figures (SEC-bench Pro, ExploitGym, CWE-bench) and NVIDIA’s leaderboard claims for Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe.
  • Secondary coverage only: Items marked “secondary coverage only” rest on news reporting, not a primary paper or lab post, and should be read accordingly.
  • Resurfacing, not a launch: EmbeddingGemma 2 is a resurfacing in the October 28 Hugging Face feed, not an October 28 launch — its original Google release was in early October.
  • Two “Memory Tax” papers: Two unrelated papers share the name “Memory Tax” — this edition covers He et al. (arXiv:2610.34073), not Peng’s LoCoMo paper (arXiv:2610.29823).
  • Index versions: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 scores are not comparable with v4 scores from earlier editions.
