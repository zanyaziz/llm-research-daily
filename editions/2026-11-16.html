Monday edition · Reliability, leaderboards & a quiet weekend

The fastest models aren't the most reliable — SO-Arena tops Friday's papers as a new study shows arena leaderboards can be gamed by up to 45 places

It was a quiet weekend for new LLM papers — no new Hugging Face Daily Papers list for Nov 14–15 had appeared at check time, and arXiv indexes still showed Nov 13 as the most recent cs.CL batch — so Friday Nov 13 carries nearly all the paper weight. Saturday and Sunday were dominated instead by lab, policy and industry stories: checkpoint forensics pointing to Blackwell GPUs, a geographically gated open-weight image model, Jensen Huang's exit from Nvidia, and a record Italian privacy fine for Meta.

Reliability vs. capability. SO-Arena (paper 1), Friday's most-upvoted paper, benchmarks fast single-pass “System One” models on 2,919 tasks and finds the capability leader ranked only seventh on reliability. Leaderboard Hacking (paper 2) makes the companion point from the other side: arena rankings themselves can be moved 15–20 places by a single manipulation strategy, and up to 45 places combined — without touching model weights.

Agents on real work, still unreliable. GDP.xlsx (paper 3) puts frontier agents on 70 professional workbooks with all-or-nothing rubrics: the best configuration passed just 38.3%. Two agent-infrastructure papers — ModHarness-RL (paper 4) and CarryOn (paper 5) — try to fix the scaffolding and context problems underneath, while a meta-analysis (paper 6) warns that most multi-agent gains vanish against compute-matched baselines.

The weekend belonged to industry. No new model releases from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI or Mistral were found in the Nov 13–15 window. The briefs below cover what did move: export-control forensics readable from public weights, MiniMax's H3-Image and its gated license, the fallout from Huang's resignation (with unconfirmed Nov 16 comeback talk), Italy's €805M fine for Meta, and Anthropic's IPO pricing.

Lead sources linked in the sections below: arXiv 2611.15777 (SO-Arena), 2611.14769 (Leaderboard Hacking), 2611.17766 (GDP.xlsx); coverage linked per brief.
