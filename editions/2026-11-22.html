Sunday edition · A historic math claim, a 3.5-hour OpenAI outage & the open-weights weekend

Claude helps crack the Riemann Hypothesis — as OpenAI goes down for 3.5 hours and Inkling 2 Mini lands on laptops

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge announced Friday that he believes he has, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis — released only as ~17 million lines of Lean code, machine-checked over the weekend but not peer-reviewed and with no human-readable proof yet. The same weekend brought a 211-minute OpenAI outage that failed 75% of requests at its peak, and Thinking Machines Lab's Inkling 2 Mini: a 26.4B open-weight looped MoE that vendor-reports 79.2% on SWE-bench Verified and runs on a 32 GB Mac.

A claim, not yet a proof. Paper 1 is the biggest story of the weekend and the least settled: Alpöge's proof exists only as 175,187 Lean modules. On Nov 21 the Lean Focused Research Organization and Mathlib Initiative announced independent verification, and MIT's Andrew Sutherland reported all modules compiling with zero errors — but that is machine-checking of the formalization, not vetting of the underlying ideas. The Clay Mathematics Institute still lists the hypothesis as unsolved, and a Stanford postdoc independently submitted a proof the same day. Read the prominent caveat on paper 1 before citing it anywhere.

Open weights and an outage. Paper 2, Inkling 2 Mini, resets the local-coding frontier: 26.4B total parameters, only 3.4B active, its middle layers looped three times per token, Apache 2.0, ~20 GB — frontier-class agentic coding on a laptop, by vendor-reported numbers. Paper 3 is the counterpoint on reliability: scheduled Postgres maintenance crashed both read replicas behind OpenAI's authentication on Saturday afternoon, taking failure rates to 75% across ChatGPT and the API for 211 minutes.

Locking down reasoning, pricing the frontier. Paper 4: OpenAI says its anti-distillation safeguards cut reasoning-extraction success from 54% to 0.2% (vendor-reported internal eval). Paper 5: Anthropic shares slid 8.5% on Friday after a 17% IPO debut pop, as the largest IPO on record closed. Below them, a CAPTCHA benchmark models still fail, a statistical test for replacing human annotators with LLM judges, diffusion LMs from NVIDIA, and decision models from Baseten.

Lead sources linked below: Scientific American and USA Today on the Riemann Hypothesis claim (secondary reporting), Weights & Takes and Futurum Group on Inkling 2 Mini, and OpenAI's own post on combatting adversarial distillation.
